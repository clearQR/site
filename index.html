Why clearQR?

QR code phishing ("quishing") bypasses traditional email security by shifting the risky click to unmanaged personal devices. clearQR keeps you protected.

Full URL Visibility

Unlike other scanners that truncate URLs, clearQR displays the complete destination so you can see exactly where you're going before you click.

Risk Score Analysis

Get an instant risk assessment with heuristic analysis detecting URL shorteners, suspicious TLDs, brand impersonation, and homoglyph attacks.

Audit Logging & SIEM

Every scan is logged with configurable retention. Forward events to your SIEM via Syslog TLS or HTTPS for security team visibility and incident response.

The Quishing Problem

Proofpoint blocked over 4.2 million QR phishing threats in H1 2025. Kaspersky saw a 5x increase in quishing emails between August and November 2025. QR codes now appear in 26% of phishing campaigns because they move the click off governed devices and onto personal mobiles where enterprise protections don't apply.
